# Jackdaw Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 13, 2026 — draft, review before store submission*

Jackdaw is a free, open-source browser extension that shows community price history for Micro Center products. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What we collect

- **A random device identifier.** Generated by your browser on install (a UUID). It is not linked to your name, email, or any account — none exist.
- **Price observations.** When you visit a Micro Center product page, the extension reads the product facts already shown to you on that page (product ID, price, stock status, your selected store number) and contributes them to the shared community database.
- **Content you choose to post.** Comments, votes, reports, and the display name you type. Display names are chosen by you and are not verified identities.
- **Price alerts you set.** The product and target price, tied to your device identifier.

## What we do NOT collect

- No email, name, address, or account of any kind.
- No browsing history. The extension activates only on `microcenter.com` product pages and observes nothing anywhere else.
- No purchases, payment data, or cart contents (the extension has no access to checkout pages).
- No ads, no analytics trackers, no sale of data — to anyone, ever.

## Where data lives

Data is stored with our backend provider (Convex, hosted in the United States) and consists solely of the items above. Aggregate price history is community property: it is displayed to all users and may be published in open formats.

## Your choices

- Uninstalling the extension deletes your device identifier from your browser; data previously contributed (price points, comments) remains part of the community record but is not linked to you in any identifying way.
- To request removal of a specific comment you posted, open an issue on our GitHub repository.

## Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at the Jackdaw GitHub repository.
