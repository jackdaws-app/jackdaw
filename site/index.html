How it works

The flock does the remembering

01

You browse, as usual

Open any Micro Center product page. Jackdaw reads the price already on your screen. Nothing is scraped, nothing is crawled.

02

Everyone's visits add up

Each visit contributes one price point to a shared history. Thousands of ordinary shopping trips become a record none of us had alone.

03

You see the whole story

A chart, a verdict, and notes from people who were just in the aisle, right on the page, before you commit to the drive.

What you get

Everything a price tag won't tell you

Price history you can read at a glance

Every point on the chart is a real shopper's visit. Hover for exact days, filter by store, and watch the amber line for open-box prices, the ones that never make it into any ad.

An alert that waits for your price

Name the number you'd actually pay. The flock keeps watching, and the moment any member sees it at or below your target, your browser tells you.
