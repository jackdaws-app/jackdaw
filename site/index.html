How it works

The flock does the remembering

01

You browse, as usual

Open a Micro Center product page, or a page of search results. Jackdaw reads the prices already on your screen. Nothing is scraped, nothing is crawled, and not one extra request is made.

02

Everyone's visits add up

A product page adds one price point. A page of results adds every card on it. Thousands of ordinary shopping trips become a record none of us had alone.

03

You see the whole story

A chart, a verdict, and notes from people who were just in the aisle—right on the page, before you commit to the drive.

Built to know nothing about you

No account. No tracking. No catch.

A price tracker that watched you would be worth less than the prices it tracked. So this one doesn't.

Read the source→
  • No sign-up. Every feature works anonymously. An optional account syncs alerts across browsers and lets you claim a handle—nothing requires it.
  • We never learn what you searched for. On a results page Jackdaw records the products that were displayed, never the query that displayed them. The address of the page is not sent at all.
  • Nowhere but Micro Center. The extension runs on product, search, and category pages and is inert everywhere else on the web.
  • No ads, no data sales. Not now, not later. The licence says so and the source proves it.
